# Brand assets used in storefront illustrations

- Social platform and product SVG marks were downloaded from Simple Icons (`https://cdn.simpleicons.org/`). The Simple Icons project publishes brand marks under its stated icon terms; each trademark remains owned by its respective company.
- The Mobile Legends: Bang Bang, Free Fire, and Roblox square game profile images are the app icons shown on their Google Play listings. They are used as small descriptive illustrations for the corresponding catalog entries.
- Game cover thumbnails use publisher images from the linked Google Play listings, an official Garena wallpaper, and Riot's VALORANT asset kit. They are stored locally and cropped only by CSS; the original assets remain unmodified.
